# Source and preparation

`reference.png` is the user-supplied travi logo, preserved unchanged.
`extracted-master.png` was created with the built-in image generation/editing tool in transparent-background edit mode. Final SVGs are simplified outlines derived from that extraction, with consistent flat periwinkle and lavender colors; PNGs are rendered from the same outlines. They are cleaned derivatives rather than original designer vector files.

The white script t is preserved inside the colored tile. White outside the logo and between letters becomes transparent. Stray disconnected edge artifacts are discarded when deriving the six intentional components: tile, r, a, v, i stem, and i dot.

## Final extraction prompt

Use case: background-extraction.
Edit target: the attached official travi logo. Produce a production-ready clean transparent PNG of EXACTLY this horizontal logo, tightly framed with a small transparent margin. Preserve every contour, proportions, spacing, the custom italic white t with long sweeping crossbar inside a periwinkle rounded square, and the matching lowercase r a v i to its right, including dot. The whole composition reads travi: the t is the white symbol INSIDE the square, then r a v i outside. DO NOT add another t outside. Remove only the white/off-white canvas/background including white areas BETWEEN the outside letters. Keep the white t INSIDE the rounded square opaque white. Preserve soft periwinkle/purple tones: tile around #909DC3, outside letters around #7D89B1. Remove paper texture/dirty white fringe, give smooth clean edges, but do not redesign or restyle any letter or symbol. No shadows, no mockup, no extra text, no checkerboard painted in. Actual transparent alpha background, landscape high resolution.
