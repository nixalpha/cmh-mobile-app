# travi — brand guidelines

## The identity

The script t in the tile is the first letter of travi. The outside lettering is ravi. Use the supplied combined artwork; never add a second t or recreate the wordmark in a font.

The original supplied logo was cleaned into transparent artwork using the built-in image tool. Clean SVG outlines and consistent flat colors were then derived from that extraction. The source reference remains in assets/brand/source/reference.png in the app repository.

## Logo choices

- Color: periwinkle tile, white script t, lavender wordmark. Use on paper or white.
- White: white silhouette with the t knocked out. Use on ink or an uncluttered dark photograph.
- Dark: ink silhouette with the t knocked out. Use for one-color printing or stronger contrast.
- Symbol: the tile and t, for avatars and compact UI. Pair with the full name somewhere nearby when introducing the product.

## Space and size

- Clear space: at least one quarter of the tile height on every side.
- Digital minimum: full lockup 65 px wide (24 px tall); symbol 24 px square.
- Print minimum: full lockup 25 mm wide; symbol 8 mm square.
- Use SVG for print or resizing. The transparent lockup PNG is 2400 px wide; symbol PNGs are 1024 px.
- Native app icons use a full-bleed background; the operating system applies its own mask. Do not use the rounded-tile PNG as an iOS store icon.

Use periwinkle tile color for button fills, selected tabs, and chips with ink text. Use the deeper indigo for small accent text and links. Ink text on periwinkle has more than 4.5:1 contrast.

## Typography and voice

Use Nunito for the app and supporting brand copy. The logo uses custom artwork, not a typeface. Use short, warm, specific language: places, memories, favorites, people. Keep the brand name lowercase.

## Avoid

Do not stretch, rotate, crop, add a shadow, recolor arbitrarily, alter the t flourish, separate the outside letters, or place the color logo over a busy photo. Use an approved white/dark version or a quiet solid backing when needed.

## Social exports

- Link preview: 1200 × 630.
- Square post: 1080 × 1080.
- Story: 1080 × 1920 (key content kept away from top/bottom UI).
- Profile header: 1500 × 500.

These graphics are reusable brand templates, not store screenshots or launch announcements.
