03 / Usage

Logo guidelines

Space

Leave at least ¼ of the tile’s height around the entire logo. Keep photos and text outside that area.

Scale

Use the lockup at 65 px wide or larger. The symbol can go down to 24 px. Use SVG when resizing or printing.

Contrast

Use color on a light, quiet surface. Use white on a dark surface, and dark for one-color printing. Keep proportions intact.

Supporting type

Nunito for headings and body copy. The logo uses custom outlines; never typeset a replacement.

Voice

Warm, direct, and personal. Say places, memories, favorites, and people. Always write travi in lowercase.
